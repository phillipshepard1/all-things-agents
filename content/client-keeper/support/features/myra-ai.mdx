# How to Use Myra, Your AI Assistant

Myra is Client Keeper's built-in AI assistant designed to streamline client data management by automating data entry tasks. You can communicate with Myra through text, photos, voice commands, or combinations thereof.

## Requirements

- Access to the Client Keeper app on a mobile device
- Pre-saved client contacts (when updating existing records)

## Accessing Myra

Tap the **Myra** icon in the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen—it resembles a simplified robot head or an "M". The main interface displays four input options:

- **Text** - Type your instructions
- **Camera** - Capture photos
- **Upload** - Select existing images
- **Audio** - Record voice commands

## Instruction Methods

### Text Commands

Enter a title and detailed message describing your request. The interface supports voice-to-text input for convenience.

### Photo Uploads

**Camera:** Capture new photos of:
- Handwritten notes
- Business cards
- Sign-in sheets

**Upload:** Select existing images from your device's library. Add a title on the "Note Group" screen.

### Audio Instructions

1. Press the green record button
2. State your request clearly
3. Stop recording when finished

### Combined Inputs (Recommended)

For optimal results, pair multiple input types together:
1. Upload a photo
2. Tap "Add More +"
3. Attach audio or text providing additional context

<Callout type="tip">
Combining a photo with voice or text instructions gives Myra better context and produces more accurate results.
</Callout>

## Submission & Completion

1. Submit your "Note Group" by selecting the **Submit** button
2. You'll receive confirmation: "MYRA Is Working Her Magic Now"
3. Processing typically takes 8-12 hours
4. Once complete, check the client's profile Notes section for updated information

## Best Practices

| Practice | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **Be specific** | Provide detailed instructions rather than vague requests |
| **Ensure clarity** | Use legible, well-lit photos of handwritten materials |
| **Verify accuracy** | Review completed entries on client profiles promptly |
