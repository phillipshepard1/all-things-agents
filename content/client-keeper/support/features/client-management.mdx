# Client Management

Client Keeper's client management system helps you keep track of every detail about your clients.

## Client Profiles

Each client profile includes:

- **Contact Details** - Phone, email, address
- **Property Preferences** - What they're looking for
- **Communication History** - Every interaction logged
- **Notes & Tags** - Custom organization
- **Documents** - Store contracts, IDs, and more

## Organizing Clients

### Tags

Use tags to categorize clients:
- Buyer / Seller
- Hot Lead / Warm Lead / Cold Lead
- First-time Buyer
- Investor
- Referral

### Smart Lists

Create filtered views based on:
- Status (Active, Past, Prospect)
- Location preferences
- Budget range
- Last contact date

## Communication Tracking

Client Keeper automatically logs:
- Emails sent through the platform
- Phone calls (when using our calling feature)
- Meeting notes
- Text messages

## Tips for Success

<Callout type="tip">
Update client notes immediately after every interaction. This habit will save you hours when you need to recall important details.
</Callout>

1. **Be consistent** - Use the same tagging system for all clients
2. **Log everything** - Even small interactions matter
3. **Review regularly** - Set aside time weekly to review your client list
