# How to Create and Manage Notes

This guide demonstrates how to create notes, format content with text and images, and organize them using the pinning feature for quick access.

## What You'll Need

- Access to the Notes section within the app

## Creating a Note

### Step 1: Start a New Note

Navigate to the **Notes** screen and select the purple plus icon in the upper right corner to open a blank note editor.

### Step 2: Add a Title and Content

Enter a note name in the title field at the top, then compose your content in the main body area below the formatting toolbar.

### Step 3: Format Your Note (Optional)

The formatting toolbar offers text customization options. Highlight text and select:

- **B** for bold
- **I** for italic
- **U** for underline

### Step 4: Add an Image

Select the image icon in the formatting toolbar, choose a photo from your device's library, and the image will be inserted into your note.

### Step 5: Save Your Note

Tap the purple **Save** button to finalize your note and return to the notes list where it will appear under the **All** tab.

## Organizing with Pins

### Pinning Important Notes

1. Locate your desired note in the list
2. Tap the bell icon on the right side
3. The bell icon will turn purple, indicating the note is now pinned

### Viewing Pinned Notes

Access the **Pinned** tab to display only your most important notes for convenient reference.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="warning">
**Save button inactive?** Make sure you have entered a title for your note, as a title is required to save.
</Callout>
