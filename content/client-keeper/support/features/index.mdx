# Client Keeper Features

Discover everything Client Keeper can do to help you manage your real estate business more effectively.

## Core Features

### Client Management
Keep all your client information organized in one place. Track preferences, communication history, and important dates.

### Deals Pipeline
Visualize your sales pipeline and track deals from initial contact to closing.

### Automated Reminders
Never miss a follow-up with smart, automated reminders based on your clients' activities and important dates.
