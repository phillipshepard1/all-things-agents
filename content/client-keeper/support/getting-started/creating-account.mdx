# Creating Your Account

Getting started with Client Keeper takes just a few minutes. Follow these simple steps to create your account.

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit [clientkeeper.com](/) and click "Start Free Trial"
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a secure password
4. Click "Create Account"

## Step 2: Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for a verification email from Client Keeper. Click the verification link to activate your account.

<Callout type="info">
Can't find the email? Check your spam folder or request a new verification link.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Complete Your Profile

Once verified, you'll be prompted to:
- Add your name
- Enter your brokerage information
- Set your timezone
- Upload a profile photo (optional)

## You're All Set!

Your account is now ready. Head to the dashboard to start adding your first clients.
