This guide helps new Client Keeper app users complete their initial login and profile setup to access the main dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- An existing Client Keeper account
- Your login credentials (username/email and password)
- Client Keeper app installed on your mobile device
- Access to your welcome email containing your temporary username

## Login Process

### Step 1: Access the Login Screen

Launch the app and locate the login option at the bottom of the welcome screen. You'll be directed to a screen featuring a purple header stating "Keep Past Clients. Nurture New Leads."

### Step 2: Submit Login Information

1. Enter your temporary username (from welcome email) or signup email address
2. Input your account password
3. Select the purple **Login** button

### Step 3: Complete Profile Information

Fill out the personal details form with:

| Field | Required |
|-------|----------|
| First and last name | Yes |
| Phone number | Yes |
| City | Yes |
| State | Yes |
| Zip code | Yes |

Select **Next** to proceed after entering all details.

### Step 4: Enable Notifications

A screen titled "Never miss a thing" will appear:

1. Tap the notification permission button
2. Approve the system-level permission request when prompted

<Callout type="tip">
Enabling notifications allows Myra (your AI assistant) to send timely reminders about follow-ups and important tasks.
</Callout>

### Step 5: Complete Setup

You'll receive a confirmation screen stating "All Done." Select **Enter App** to access your Follow Up dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="warning">
**Login failed?** Verify your credentials are correct. Use the password recovery option if needed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
**Missing username?** Check your email spam folder for the welcome message.
</Callout>
