# Adding Your First Client

Now that your account is set up, let's add your first client to Client Keeper.

## Quick Add

The fastest way to add a client:

1. Click the **+ Add Client** button in your dashboard
2. Enter the client's name and email
3. Click **Save**

That's it! Your client is now in the system.

## Detailed Client Profile

For a more complete client record, you can add:

### Contact Information
- Phone number(s)
- Email address
- Mailing address
- Preferred contact method

### Property Preferences
- Budget range
- Preferred locations
- Property type (single family, condo, etc.)
- Must-have features
- Deal breakers

### Important Dates
- Birthday
- Anniversary
- Move-in deadline
- Contract expiration dates

## Importing Existing Clients

Have clients in a spreadsheet? You can import them in bulk:

1. Go to **Settings > Import**
2. Download our CSV template
3. Fill in your client data
4. Upload the completed file

<Callout type="tip">
Pro tip: Start with your most active clients and add the rest over time. You don't need to import everyone at once!
</Callout>
