# How to Export Your Contact Data

This guide explains how to export contact information from the Client Keeper app. You can export all data at once or specific lists based on events like birthdays or housiversaries. The data will be sent to your email as a CSV file, which can be opened with spreadsheet programs like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

## Requirements

- Access to the Client Keeper app on a mobile device
- Saved contacts in the app
- For event-based exports, relevant information (birthdays/housiversaries) must be saved for contacts

## Export Instructions

### Step 1: Navigate to Contacts

Open the app and select the two-person icon in the bottom navigation bar to access your contacts list.

### Step 2: Access the Export Menu

Tap the three-dots menu icon located in the top right corner of the Contacts screen. A menu will appear at the bottom of the screen.

### Step 3: Select Export CSV

Choose **Export CSV** from the menu to proceed to the export configuration screen.

### Step 4: Choose Your Export Type

#### All Data (Default)

Leave the Event dropdown set to "All Data" to export your entire contact database.

#### Event-Based Exports

For targeted exports based on dates:

1. Select **Birthdays** or **Housiversaries** from the Event dropdown
2. Tap "Pick a Date" to select your desired date range using the calendar
3. Confirm your selection

<Callout type="info">
Event-based exports are perfect for sending birthday cards or anniversary greetings to clients within a specific time period.
</Callout>

#### Group-Specific Export (Optional)

Enable the toggle for "Export a Groups Data" to export only a specific contact group.

### Step 5: Complete the Export

1. Tap the **Export** button
2. Enter your email address when prompted
3. A CSV file containing your selected data will be sent to your email inbox shortly

## What to Do with Your Export

- **Mail merges** - Import into your email marketing tool
- **Marketing campaigns** - Target specific client segments
- **Birthday notifications** - Plan ahead for client outreach
- **Backup** - Keep a copy of your contact information
