# Troubleshooting

Having issues? Find solutions to common problems below.

## Login Issues

### I forgot my password

1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for reset instructions

<Callout type="warning">
Password reset links expire after 24 hours. Request a new one if yours has expired.
</Callout>

### I'm not receiving emails from Client Keeper

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Add support@clientkeeper.com to your contacts
3. Verify your email address is correct in settings

## Performance Issues

### The app is loading slowly

Try these steps:

1. Clear your browser cache
2. Disable browser extensions temporarily
3. Try a different browser
4. Check your internet connection

### Data isn't syncing

1. Refresh the page
2. Log out and log back in
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact support if the issue persists

## Mobile App Issues

### The app keeps crashing

1. Update to the latest version
2. Restart your phone
3. Uninstall and reinstall the app
4. Check for iOS/Android system updates

### Push notifications aren't working

1. Check notification settings in the app
2. Verify phone settings allow notifications
3. Log out and log back in

## Still Need Help?

If you can't find a solution here:

1. **Live Chat** - Available Mon-Fri, 9am-6pm EST
2. **Email** - support@clientkeeper.com
3. **Phone** - 1-800-CLIENT (Pro and Team plans)

We typically respond within 24 hours.
