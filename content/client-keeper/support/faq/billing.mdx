# Billing & Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about Client Keeper pricing and billing.

## Pricing Plans

### What plans are available?

We offer three plans:

| Plan | Price | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | $29/month | Solo agents |
| Professional | $49/month | Growing businesses |
| Team | $99/month | Brokerages & teams |

### Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes! Save 20% when you pay annually instead of monthly.

### Can I switch plans?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

## Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?

- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- PayPal
- Bank transfer (annual plans only)

### When am I charged?

You're charged on the same date each month (or year for annual plans) that you originally subscribed.

### Can I get a refund?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all new subscriptions. Contact support within 14 days for a full refund.

## Cancellation

### How do I cancel my subscription?

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Follow the prompts

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your data anytime before then, or reactivate your account to restore access.
