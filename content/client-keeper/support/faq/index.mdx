# Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about Client Keeper.

## General Questions

### What is Client Keeper?

Client Keeper is a CRM (Customer Relationship Management) tool built specifically for real estate agents. It helps you manage clients, track deals, and never miss a follow-up.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes! We offer a 30-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required to start.

### What happens after my trial ends?

You'll be prompted to choose a plan. If you decide not to continue, your data will be safely stored for 30 days in case you change your mind.
