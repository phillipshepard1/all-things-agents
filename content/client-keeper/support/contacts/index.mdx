# Managing Contacts & Follow-Ups

Client Keeper makes it easy to manage your client relationships. This section covers everything you need to know about adding contacts, setting up follow-up reminders, and organizing your contacts into groups.

## Quick Tips

- Always add at least an email or phone number when creating contacts
- Set up recurring follow-ups immediately after adding a new contact
- Use groups to segment your contacts for targeted outreach
