This guide explains how to establish automated reminder intervals for staying connected with your contacts in Client Keeper.

## Requirements

You must have at least one contact already saved in your Client Keeper app.

## Setting Up Recurring Follow-Ups

### Step 1: Navigate to Contacts

Access the Contacts tab using the two-person icon in the bottom navigation bar.

### Step 2: Open a Contact Profile

Select a contact from your list to view their detailed information.

### Step 3: Access Follow-Up Settings

In the contact's detail page, find the **Follow Ups** section and tap the arrow icon to launch the editor.

### Step 4: Set Reminder Frequency

Open the Follow-up Frequency dropdown and choose your preferred interval:

| Frequency | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| 2 Weeks | Hot leads, active deals |
| 4 Weeks | Warm leads |
| 6 Weeks | Regular check-ins |
| Quarterly (12 Weeks) | Past clients, sphere of influence |
| 6 Months | Annual touchpoints |

### Step 5: Select Contact Method

From the Follow-up Method dropdown, specify how you prefer to reach out:

- Phone call
- Text message
- Email

### Step 6: Confirm Your Settings

Click the purple **Save** button to apply your follow-up schedule.

### Step 7: Monitor Scheduled Reminders

Navigate to the Follow Up tab and select "Coming Up" to view your scheduled contacts and their reminder dates.

## Ongoing Management

Your reminders automatically transition to the "This Week" tab as their scheduled dates approach, ensuring you stay on track with client outreach.

<Callout type="tip">
Start with a 6-week interval for most contacts. You can always adjust the frequency based on how engaged the client is.
</Callout>

## Best Practices

1. **Match frequency to relationship** - Active buyers need more frequent contact
2. **Use consistent methods** - Some clients prefer calls, others prefer text
3. **Review weekly** - Check your "This Week" tab every Monday
4. **Adjust as needed** - Update frequency based on client engagement
